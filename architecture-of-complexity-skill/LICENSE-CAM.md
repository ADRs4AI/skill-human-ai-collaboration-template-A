## What is CAM?

CAM (Computationally-Assisted Metacognition) is a pedagogical framework for
external metacognitive support through computational partnership. It conceptualizes
AI as a "System 3" that operates as an external metacognitive layer, separate
from but complementary to human System 1 (automatic) and System 2 (deliberate)
thinking.
